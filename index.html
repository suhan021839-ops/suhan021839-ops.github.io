Confirm the channel

Manage uploads

Checks local video files, prevents duplicate queue entries and resumes interrupted transfers.

Keep control

Upload and public-publishing controls are separate. Initial uploads are configured as private.

How access works

This application uses YouTube API Services. It requests youtube.readonly to identify the owner’s channel and check video processing status, and youtube.upload to submit approved videos and their metadata. Google handles sign-in; the application does not collect the Google account password.

The uploader runs on the owner’s PC. This website provides product and privacy information; it does not sign visitors into Google or accept video uploads. The application is currently for its owner’s personal use and is not offered as a public multi-user service.

Current scope

The current version is an upload and queue-management tool. Automatic story writing, video generation and third-party AI integrations are not yet connected to this uploader. Their data practices will be documented before they are enabled.
